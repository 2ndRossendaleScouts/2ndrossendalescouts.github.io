About us

Rooted in Rossendale

The group was formed in Bacup in 1910, just a few years after the beginning of the Scout movement. Music became a major part of the group when the Scout Band was formed in 1963.

Today the headquarters supports weekly Scouting, music lessons, rehearsals and performances for children, young people and adult musicians.
